## In My Own Words

**What is the cybersecurity landscape, and why does it matter?**

```
The cybersecurity landscape tells you exactly what and who are involved in the cybersecurity environment. The landscape consists of attackers, defenders, everyday users and the digital infrastructure. The landscape matters because everyone plays a role in how the digital infrastructure is secured. 
```

**How do hardware, OS, and software layers interact?**

```
The hardware is the physical part of a computer which houses the RAM, Storage, Motherboard, CPU and devices. The operating system such as Windows utilizes the resources (data/files) found in the hardware to make sure the apps on the software layer are able to make requests and work properly.
```

**One term I want to remember from this week, and why:**

```
Attack surface- The attack surface which is every possible entry point that an attacker can access a device or system. 
I want to remember this term because it reminds me that every device that I add and/or every app that I download could possibly be a point of access for an attacker. The more devices/apps the bigger the attack surface.
```
